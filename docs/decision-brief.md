# Decision Brief: Engage v2 — For Marcus

## Situation
30-day retention has dropped from 44% to 37% over the last two quarters, driven by users going passive after the initial spending-breakdown "aha moment." Three independent research sources — user interviews, NPS feedback, and a competitive scan — converge on the same root cause: the app doesn't change or guide users after week 1.

## Key Findings
- The app's experience is static after first use — the top-ranked theme in both NPS feedback (3 of 10 comments) and user interviews; users report "nothing has changed since the first day."
- Users want direction, not just data — multiple users explicitly ask for a "coach," not a "tracker," to tell them what to do next.
- Personalization breaks down fast — a stated savings goal was never referenced again, and nudges felt random enough that one user disabled notifications entirely.
- Time-to-value may be the real churn driver — our power user said it took ~3 months for the app to start feeling personalized, and that "most people give up before that" — consistent with our churned user leaving at week 5.
- No competitor (Monarch Money, Cleo, Rocket Money) proactively delivers one ranked, personalized weekly insight, and none solve the email-to-in-app continuity problem — both are open white space, not a "catching up to competitors" play.

## Options Considered
1. **Ship the proposed personalized weekly summary screen** (top insight + one actionable nudge + savings goal progress) — directly addresses the static-experience and lack-of-guidance themes, and occupies white space competitors don't own.
2. **Fix personalization gaps first** (surface goal progress, smarter nudge targeting) as a faster, lower-lift improvement before building new insight-ranking logic.
3. **Do nothing new and only optimize onboarding** — rejected: research shows the drop-off happens after the aha moment, not during it; onboarding is not where the problem lives.

## Recommended Action
Move forward with the personalized weekly summary screen as the Engage v2 direction, sequencing the low-lift personalization fixes (goal progress, nudge targeting) as a first milestone within it.

## Why Now
Retention is actively declining (44%→37%), the research converges from three independent sources on the same cause, and the time-to-value finding suggests every quarter of delay is costing us users who churn before they'd ever see the payoff — the window to act is closing, not opening.
