# PRD Skeleton: Re-engaging Passive Users

## Problem Statement
30-day retention has dropped from 44% to 37% quarter-over-quarter. Churn is sharpest among users who don't set a savings goal in week 1 (nearly double the churn rate vs. those who do). User research suggests that after the initial dashboard/spending breakdown experience, users don't have a clear next step. The home feed looks identical regardless of how long it's been since the user last opened the app, giving them no reason to come back. The weekly summary email has a decent 22% open rate, but clicking through leads to an app experience that doesn't continue the story the email started, which feels jarring. Hypothesis: users go passive because the app stops feeling relevant after the first "aha moment," and there's nothing pulling them back with a reason specific to them.

## Goals
- Give users a reason to return to the app that is personalized to their own data/patterns, not generic tips.
- Continue the narrative started by the weekly summary email into the in-app experience.
- Surface a personalized weekly summary screen showing: top insight for the week, one actionable nudge based on actual usage patterns, and progress toward savings goal (if one is set).
